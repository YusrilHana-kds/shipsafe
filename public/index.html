The honest part

Your checklist passed.
Your bank still rejected it.

A generic compliance checklist tells you what documents to get. It doesn't tell you that the consignee name on your Bill of Lading has one extra word compared to your Invoice — which is exactly what banks reject under UCP 600 Art. 14(d).

What other tools say
"Get a Bill of Lading from your shipping line."
What ShipSafe says
"Your BL shows consignee as 'Global Harvest Trading Co.' but your LC says 'Global Harvest Trading'. Under UCP 600 Art. 14(d), this is a discrepancy. Bank will reject. Fix before sailing."
What other tools say
"Lab test your cocoa for aflatoxin."
What ShipSafe says
"FDA limit is 20 ppb. Japan limit is 10 ppb. Test 3 weeks before sailing — SGS or Bureau Veritas. Aflatoxin rises during transit in humid containers. Add desiccants."
