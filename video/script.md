# ShipSafe walkthrough — on-screen script

75 s, no voiceover. Each scene shows a step label (top) and one explanatory subtitle (bottom).
Edit the text in `video/walkthrough.html` (`CAPTIONS` and `SUBS`), then run
`node video/render.mjs && python3 video/sound.py`.

| Time | Step label | Subtitle |
|------|------------|----------|
| 0 – 6 s | — (hook) | One wrong document can hold your container at port — and cost you hundreds in bank fees. |
| 6 – 14 s | 01 · Pick your corridor | With ShipSafe, just pick your product, origin, destination and Incoterm. No guesswork. |
| 14 – 25 s | 02 · Get your checklist | In seconds, you get every rule that applies — straight from official sources. Plus the blind spots most exporters miss. |
| 25 – 37 s | 03 · Cross-check your docs | Upload your trade documents. ShipSafe checks every field against your letter of credit and flags what your bank would reject — before you sail. |
| 37 – 45 s | 04 · Know your landed cost | See your full landed cost by Incoterm — and your real margin — before you quote. |
| 45 – 53 s | 05 · Ask Tara anything | Stuck on a question? Ask Tara, your AI trade compliance assistant, for a clear, sourced answer. |
| 53 – 59 s | 06 · Trade glossary | Look up any Incoterm or banking rule in the built-in glossary. |
| 59 – 67 s | 07 · Track every shipment | Track every shipment in one place — readiness, documents and deadlines at a glance. |
| 67 – 75 s | — (outro) | Logo · "Know it. Check it. Ship it." · Check my corridor → |

Soundtrack: original background music + UI sound effects (clicks, checks, alerts, chime), generated by `video/sound.py`.
